02 / MP3

Play your own MP3s offline

Choose Add files to add MP3s from your phone and play them offline.

Low on storage? Choose Add link and paste a Google Drive link to an MP3 file or a folder. Sonacella builds a playlist from the folder's MP3s, so you can stream your collection over Wi-Fi without copying it to your phone.

Use a folder shared with anyone who has the link, with downloads allowed. Tap Refresh after adding new files to your Google Drive folder.

03 / Chapters

Find your place in a long recording

Add a matching timing file to split a long MP3 into named chapters. Jump straight to a song or section and resume from your saved position. Learn how to split a long MP3 into tracks.

In Playlists, choose Import Drive folder and paste a shared Google Drive folder link. Sonacella automatically creates a playlist from its MP3s and splits recordings into chapters when matching timing files are available.

Send a radio station to your other devices

Connect the same Google account on each device in Settings → Device sync. While listening, open the RADIO menu and tap Sync station. Your other devices can switch to that station if it’s already in their lists.

Each device joins the live stream at its own time.
