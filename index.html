A place for every mood:

Radio

Organise and play internet radio

Make the station list your own. Add stations by HTTPS stream or M3U playlist link, then edit their names, logos, and descriptions.

Organise stations into collapsible groups for seasons, genres, or moods. Move several stations at once while your music keeps playing.

MP3

Play your own MP3s offline

Choose Add files to add MP3s from your phone and play them offline.

Low on storage? Choose Add link and paste a Google Drive link to an MP3 file or a folder. Sonacella builds a playlist from the folder's MP3s, so you can stream your collection over Wi-Fi without copying it to your phone.

Use a folder shared with anyone who has the link, with downloads allowed. Tap Refresh after adding new files to your Google Drive folder. To take the music offline, tap the download counter beside the playlist name and choose Download playlist.

Chapters

Find your place in a long recording

Add a matching timing file to split a long MP3 into named chapters. Jump straight to a song or section and resume from your saved position. Learn how to split a long MP3 into tracks.

In Playlists, choose Import Drive folder and paste a shared Google Drive folder link. Sonacella automatically creates a playlist from its MP3s and splits recordings into chapters when matching timing files are available.

Airplane mode. Music on.

Sonacella 1.0.12

Love this track? Catch it next time.

Hear a track you want to keep on internet radio? In RADIO, open Bookmarks → Bookmark current fragment, choose Record and tap Save.

When the station plays that track again and Sonacella recognizes it, the app can record it. Open Bookmarks → Recordings and tap Play to listen from the beginning in MP3 mode.

Sonacella 1.0.11

Set a timer. Drift off.

Open Sleep timer from the menu in RADIO or MP3. Choose a preset or enter your own duration. Turn on Fade out to let the music gradually go quiet.

Tap the hourglass countdown to change or cancel the timer. When time runs out, Sonacella stops the radio or pauses your MP3 and saves your place.

Sonacella 1.0.9

From a station website to your radio list

Found a radio station on the web? Open its page in Microsoft Edge and use Sonacella Station Cards to create a station card. Review the stream, artwork and details, then display its QR code.

In Sonacella, open Add stations → From QR code. Scan the code from your browser with the camera, or choose a saved QR image. Preview the station and confirm to add it to your list.

You can also add copied station cards with From clipboard. Sonacella checks new audio sources before saving. Device sync and switching conditions now live together in Settings.

Station Cards 0.1.4 · Edge extension

Found a station? Take it with you.

The standalone Sonacella Station Cards preview is now available on GitHub. Turn a supported station website into a reviewed card with an audio source, artwork and details, then save JSON or a local QR image.

Version 0.1.4 supports published HTTPS stream ports and Classic Radio Non Stop, alongside supported Klassik Radio channels. The download includes installation and manual-update instructions, with a separate SHA-256 file.

Good radio is worth sharing

Share a station from its card, hold a group heading to share a group, or choose Share station list in the RADIO menu to send your full list through your favourite messenger.

Open the attachment in Sonacella to preview a station's artwork and details. Tap Add shared station, or choose which stations to add from a shared group or list.

Sonacella 1.0.7

Send a radio station to your other devices

Connect the same Google account on each device in Settings → Device sync. While listening, open the RADIO menu and tap Sync station. Your other devices can switch to that station if it’s already in their lists.

Each device joins the live stream at its own time.
