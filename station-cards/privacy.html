Privacy

Sonacella Station Cards · Version 0.1.4 · Last updated November 12, 2026

Your chosen station

The extension reads the active tab URL only when you open its toolbar popup. It does not monitor browsing, inject page scripts, read your phone catalog or access your YouTube or Google Drive accounts.

Website checks

After you allow HTTPS website access, the extension requests the station page you choose, its supported public player configuration, a bounded stream sample and optional artwork. Website operators receive normal network information such as your IP address and the requested address. Requests omit browser cookies, saved authorization and referrer information. Your browser's proxy, DNS and network configuration still applies. HTTPS redirects follow browser rules; intermediate hops and resolved IP addresses are not independently checked. Local hostnames and literal private/reserved IP addresses are rejected, but this is not a network firewall. Use trusted public station pages.

Permissions and control

The optional HTTPS permission covers all HTTPS sites because official station sources, players and redirected streaming hosts differ between stations. The extension requests it explicitly before checking a station. Remove website access on the review page or in Edge extension settings. Removing access clears the current result in open review tabs. Closing a review tab ends its check; no background job is created.

Local data and exports

Card results stay in the review tab's memory. The extension has no analytics, developer backend, AI service, persistent station history or account system. QR codes are generated locally with bundled software. JSON, verification reports and PNG images are saved only when you choose their download actions. Clipboard copy and optional system file sharing happen only when selected. Files you save or share are controlled by you and your selected destination; removing access does not delete them.

Provider conditions

Public access does not establish third-party use, redistribution or recording rights. Review the provider's published conditions. Stream checks verify sample content, not Android playback or future availability.

Support and privacy contact

Contact ceziawaiting@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. If you send a message or diagnostic report, the details and attachments you choose to provide are used to answer your request. Review reports before sending: they can contain station URLs and provider redirect parameters. Do not send passwords, private links or other information you do not want to disclose. Your email provider processes the communication under its own policy.

Retention and your choices

Closing the review tab discards its in-memory card result. The website access grant is managed by Microsoft Edge and can persist until you remove it in the extension or Edge settings. Removing access or uninstalling does not delete exports or messages you have already saved or sent. Manage those copies at their destination. The developer has no server copy of your station card to delete. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to answer your inquiry and meet applicable obligations; request its deletion through the contact above. Station and streaming operators control their own records.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated when the extension's data handling changes. Its date identifies the current text. This policy covers Sonacella Station Cards for Microsoft Edge; the Sonacella Android app has its own privacy policy.
