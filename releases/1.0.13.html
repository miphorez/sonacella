Sonacella 1.0.13

Download linked MP3 tracks or entire playlists, including tracks from public Google Drive links, and listen offline.

See download progress and continue in the background. Save offline copies to Android Downloads or a folder you choose, and move them later without downloading them again.

Check marks beside filenames show which tracks are ready to play offline. A new playlist menu groups playlist actions.
